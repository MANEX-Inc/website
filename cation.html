      FAQ
      
Questions

      

        

          Is it free?
          
Yes. Each account gets one free key. Everyone shares one daily allowance, so the service can pause when it runs out.

        

        

          Can I have more than one key?
          
One key per account. You can replace it at any time, and the old key stops working right away.

        

        

          What if I lose my key?
          
Sign in again and choose Replace my key. Keys are shown only once, so save yours when it appears.

        

        

          What do you store about me?
          
Your account ID, your username, and a hash of your key. This browser keeps a sign-in token until you sign out.

        

        

          Can I use it in my bot?
          
Yes. Call the endpoint from your own server or agent, and keep your key private. Don't put it in public website code.
