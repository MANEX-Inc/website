Privacy

How MANEX Labs handles information when you use this website and our apps.

Draft. This page needs review and details from the team before it is published.

Information we collect

MANEX doesn't collect data across any of its apps.

ContextOS

ContextOS stores the projects, tasks, notes, and other information you enter in a local database on your own device. MANEX does not receive or have access to this data, and the app does not run analytics or advertising. Exporting or backing up your data saves files wherever you choose.

The Research tab sends your searches to Google, and pages you open come from the websites you visit. Those services handle the information under their own privacy policies. Pages may store cookies in the app's private browser storage on your device, which you can clear by removing the app's data.

This website

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, the community page embeds a Discord widget, and links to GitHub and the Microsoft Store. Those services have their own privacy policies.
