Privacy

This policy applies to every MANEX Labs app and to this website. Last updated 8 November 2026.

Draft. This page needs review and details from the team before it is published.

Scope

This policy covers all MANEX Labs apps, including Surfer, ContextOS, DevDen, Volt and any other app we publish, as well as this website. Where an app has specific behaviour, it is described below.

Data MANEX collects

MANEX doesn't collect data across any of its apps.

Data stored on your device

Apps keep what you create, such as projects, notes, and settings, on your own device. MANEX doesn't receive or have access to this data. Deleting the app or its data removes it from your device.

Third-party services

Some features connect to services outside MANEX, and those services handle information under their own privacy policies:

  • Web search and pages opened in browsing features. ContextOS's Research tab sends searches to Google, and pages come from the websites you visit. Pages may store cookies in the app's private browser storage, which you can clear by removing the app's data.
  • Sign-in through GitHub or a passkey, where the provider handles the sign-in.
  • The Microsoft Store, which handles installs and updates for apps distributed there.

This website

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, the community page embeds a Discord widget, and it links to GitHub and the Microsoft Store. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

Our apps and this website are not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
