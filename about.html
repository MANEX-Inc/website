About

The people behind MANEX Inc.

A small team building practical tools, with a few partners along the way.

MANEX Inc. was founded on 20 November 2020 by two best friends who wanted to build small, useful tools that people actually enjoy using.

Over six years, that idea has grown into a range of desktop apps and an AI assistant called Volt. Along the way we have learned that simple, dependable software matters more than feature lists, and that a small team can build things people rely on.
